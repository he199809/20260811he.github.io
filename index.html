About

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Nagoya University, advised by Professor Lars Hesselholt. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in September 2027.

My research is in higher algebra and geometry, with a particular focus on algebraic K-theory, stable ∞-categories, and sheaf-theoretic methods.

Department of Mathematics, Nagoya University, Japan

Research Interests

  • Efimov's continuous algebraic K-theory and localizing motives
  • Topological Hochschild homology (THH) and topological cyclic homology (TC)
  • Higher algebra, ∞-categories, ∞-topoi, and locally rigid ∞-categories
  • Six-functor formalism, Gestalt, and light condensed anima
  • Algebraic geometry, motivic cohomology, and prismatic cohomology
